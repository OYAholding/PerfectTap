PerfectTap offers four consumable coin packs and five non-consumables: Remove Forced Ads and four cosmetic skins. There are no subscriptions, no loot boxes and no random rewards for money.

HOW TO TEST PURCHASES (Sandbox)
Purchases are enabled for the App Review / TestFlight Sandbox environment of this build. No demo account is needed; use your Sandbox Apple Account when StoreKit asks.
1. Launch the app and finish the short username setup if shown.
2. Remove Forced Ads: tap SHOP, then BUY COINS; Remove Forced Ads is listed below the coin packs. It can be bought as a guest, without creating an account.
3. Cosmetic skins: tap SHOP, then the PREMIUM tab. Skins can be bought as a guest.
4. Coin packs: tap SHOP, then BUY COINS. Coin balances are not restored by StoreKit, so before the first coin purchase the app asks you to Sign in with Apple (one tap) so the balance can be recovered on another device. After signing in, the purchase sheet opens and the coins are added once the purchase is confirmed.
5. Restore Purchases is in the PREMIUM tab and in Settings. After deleting and reinstalling, it restores Remove Forced Ads and skins from Apple's purchase history, also for guests.

ADS
Remove Forced Ads permanently removes automatic interstitial and banner ads. Clearly labeled rewarded ads remain available after the purchase; they are always optional, only shown when the player taps them, have daily limits and are never required to play, progress or finish any mode.

WHAT EACH PRODUCT DOES
Coins buy cosmetic items in the in-game shop only. Skins change colors and effects only; timing zones, indicator geometry, scoring and difficulty are unchanged. Every purchase is verified with Apple's signed transaction before it is delivered. Cloud Backup is optional and not required for Remove Forced Ads or skins.

# PerfectTap — positioning and copy (English)

**Line:** *A timing game designed with ADHD players in mind. Made for everyone.*

## The rules for every piece of copy

Rules are from [docs/research/COMPLIANCE.md](../research/COMPLIANCE.md): Apple 1.4.1 / 2.3 / 2.3.14, the FTC, Meta Personal Attributes and Health & Wellness.

| Say | Never say |
|---|---|
| designed with ADHD players in mind | for ADHD, ADHD training, helps ADHD |
| short rounds, clear feedback, you choose the pace | improves focus / attention / concentration |
| calm option: fewer effects, Reduce Motion respected | trains your brain, clinically proven, doctor-recommended |
| timing consistency (a game statistic) | attention score, focus score, ADHD score |
| "we'd like to study whether practice carries over. We don't know yet" | any result we have not measured |

A disclaimer does not fix a contradictory claim. Don't make the claim in the first place.

"Built with feedback from ADHD players" may be used **only** once that feedback is documented: who, when, and what changed.

## App Store

- **Subtitle (30 chars):** `Tap at the perfect moment`
- **Promotional text:** New in 1.2: Practice Session (same pace, same length, every time), a Progress screen for your timing, a Visual Effects setting and four new skins.
- **Description:**

> PerfectTap is a one-button timing game: tap when the bar hits the centre.
>
> • 15 ways to play, from calm (Zen, Minimal) to intense (Rush, Sudden Death)
> • Practice Session: a fixed 1-, 3- or 5-minute round at the same pace every time, so you can see your own timing improve in the game
> • Progress: your timing spread and early/late tendency over sessions
> • Visual Effects: Full, Reduced or Off. Respects iOS Reduce Motion
> • Short rounds, clear feedback, no punishing streaks
> • Cosmetic themes and effects. They never change how the game plays
>
> Designed with ADHD players in mind, made for everyone.
> PerfectTap is a game, not a medical product. Its statistics describe your play in PerfectTap only.

- **Keywords (no health terms):** timing,reflex,tap,one button,arcade,casual,rhythm,precision,calm,minimal
- **Category:** Games › Arcade (unchanged)

## Community posts

**Indie / dev communities (r/iOSGaming, r/indiegames, TouchArcade):**
> I made PerfectTap, a one-button timing game. A lot of my design choices came from thinking about players with ADHD: short rounds, instant feedback, a calm mode with effects off, no streak punishments. In 1.2 I added a fixed "Practice Session" and a progress screen that shows your timing consistency. It's a game, not therapy. I'd love feedback on whether the calm settings feel right.

**ADHD communities.** Only where self-promotion is allowed. Read the rules first and ask a moderator if unsure:
> Not a treatment and not a "brain trainer". Just a game I designed to be easy to pick up and put down: one button, rounds from 30 seconds, effects you can turn off. If you try it, tell me what's annoying. That's the feedback I'm after.

## Meta ads

- **Targeting:** broad, or casual / arcade games interests. **No** health interests (Meta no longer offers them). No custom audiences built from health data.
- **Events and conversions:** never name events `adhd_*`, `focus_*` or `diagnosis`. Never send any self-reported diagnosis.

| Use | Why it's OK |
|---|---|
| "One button. Perfect timing. How close can you get?" | Describes the game |
| "Short rounds. Clear feedback. Play for 30 seconds or 30 minutes." | Design features |
| "Turn the effects down. Keep the challenge." | Feature, no promise |
| "A timing game designed with ADHD players in mind." | Design statement, not addressed to the viewer |

| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| "Do you have ADHD?" / "Your ADHD brain will love this" | Asserts a personal attribute (Meta) |
| "Struggling to focus? Train your attention" | Implies the viewer's condition, unsubstantiated claim |
| "Clinically proven" / "Reduce symptoms" | FTC, FDA device territory, Apple 2.3.14 |
